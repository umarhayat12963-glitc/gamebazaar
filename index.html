📖 Game Guides — Zyada Score Kaise Banayein?

Sirf khelna hi mazaa nahi, jeetna us se bara mazaa hai! Neeche hamari mashhoor games ki mukhtasar guides di gayi hain. In tips par amal karein aur apne doston se zyada score bana kar dikhayein.

🐍 Snake Classic — Lamba Saanp, Bara Score

Snake duniya ki sab se purani aur pasandeeda games me se hai. Is me aap ka saanp seb kha kar lamba hota jata hai, aur jitna lamba saanp, utna zyada score. Asli mushkil ye hai ke lamba saanp apne hi jism se takra kar khatam ho sakta hai. Jeetne ki trick ye hai: hamesha kinaron (edges) ke qareeb ghumo aur beech me khaali jagah rakho taake phasne ki surat me nikalne ka rasta mile. Jaldi jaldi mod (turn) lene se bacho — ek ghalat mod aur game over. Shuru me araam se khelo, jab saanp lamba ho jaye tab aur zyada dhyaan do, kyunki asal imtehan wahi hota hai.

🔢 2048 Puzzle — Dimag Ki Asli Game

2048 me same number wali tiles ko mila mila kar bara number banana hota hai, aur aakhir me 2048 wali tile banana jeet hai. Is game ki sab se mashhoor strategy "corner strategy" hai: apni sab se bari tile ko kisi ek kone (corner) me rakho aur use wahan se hilne na do. Tiles hamesha do hi simton (directions) me sarakao, teesri simt tabhi use karo jab majboori ho. Choti tiles ko bari tile ke aas paas jama hone do — jaise hi do same number milen, wo jud kar bari tile ke qareeb bari tile bana dengi. Jo log bina soche chaaron taraf tiles sarakate hain, un ki board jaldi bhar jati hai aur game khatam ho jati hai. Sabr hi is game ki kunji hai.

🏏 Cricket Timing — Chhakka Kaise Marein?

Cricket Timing me sirf 6 balls milti hain, aur har ball par sahi lamhe par SWING dabana hota hai. Boht jaldi swing kiya to ball miss, boht der ki to bowled! Chhakka usi waqt lagta hai jab ball bat ke bilkul qareeb ho — na aage, na peeche. Pehli ek do balls par timing samajhne ki koshish karein, phir baqi balls par puray aitmaad se khelein. Yaad rakhein: ek over me 36 runs tak ban sakte hain, to har ball qeemti hai. Agar bowled ho jayein to ghabrayein nahi — ↻ dabayein aur nayi innings me apna record tor dein.

Ye GameBazaar ki khaas game hai. Bus teesri lane se shuru hoti hai aur aap ko LEFT aur RIGHT buttons se lane badal kar aati garion se bachna hota hai. 🧱 Block Tetris — Line Kaise Saaf Karein?

Tetris me girte hue blocks ko is tarah lagana hota hai ke ek poori horizontal line ban jaye — line bante hi wo gayab ho jati hai aur score milta hai. Sab se bari ghalti blocks ke beech khaali suraakh (gap) chhorna hai, kyunki suraakh ke neeche wali line kabhi poori nahi hoti. Hamesha satah (surface) hamwaar rakhein, unchi neechi tower na banayein. Lambi wali chaar blocks ki salai (I-piece) ke liye ek kone me jagah khaali rakhein — us se ek saath zyada lines saaf hoti hain aur score tezi se barhta hai.
